Our story

A specialist firm by design, not by accident.

Founder James Leigh spent his early career in specialist energy recruitment, watching critical roles go to generalist agencies running broad, contingent-style searches. They filled badly, or not at all.

Penleigh does the opposite: retained engagements only, one sector, and senior expertise on every search. Our network of 6,000+ contacts across 21 countries has been built role by role over more than a decade.

That depth is why most of our clients come back, and why we turn down roles outside our scope.

“If we don’t already know who the right ten people in the world are for your role — or know how to find them within a fortnight — you should hire someone else.”

How we work

A few things you should know up front.

  1. Retained only

    We don’t take contingent work. Its economics reward speed over fit.

  2. One sector, deeply

    Pipeline services, pipeline construction, NDT, subsea inspection and I&M robotics. We don’t take roles outside this scope.

  3. Senior-led, on every search

    No junior handover and no off-the-shelf longlists. The person you brief runs your search.

  4. HireInsight on every shortlist

    Video introduction, psychometric profile, competency assessment, references and pay expectations for every candidate, as standard.

  5. Honest about scope

    If a brief isn’t right for us, we’ll say so and point you to someone better placed.
