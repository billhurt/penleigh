Retained search for roles you can’t afford to get wrong.

Senior-led and exclusive, with no contingent racing. Shortlists typically in 3–5 weeks.

Why retained

Most senior hires don’t come from job boards.

They come from relationships, market knowledge and a process built to surface fit, not just availability.

Working retained and exclusively gives a senior search the time and focus it needs. In midstream and subsea inspection, where the talent pool is small and rarely on the market, that’s the difference.

Contingent recruitment

Pay on placement

  • The same role goes to several agencies at once
  • Recruiters are rewarded for sending CVs fast, not assessing them
  • Only reaches people already on the market
  • If it doesn’t fill, the recruiter walks away
Retained search

Pay in stages, exclusive partnership

  • One firm, fully committed to your role
  • Headhunts people who aren’t actively looking
  • HireInsight assessment on every shortlist
  • Replacement to the value of the fee if a placement leaves within 8 weeks
The method

A five-step process, built around fit.

  1. Understand

    A proper briefing on culture, success criteria and what great looks like. We’ll push back if the brief doesn’t match the market.

    Week 1

  2. Source

    We headhunt, starting from 6,000+ sector contacts plus mapped research. Every approach is direct and named.

    Weeks 1–3

  3. Assess in HireInsight

    Shortlisted candidates come with video, psychometrics, competency results, pay expectations, references and CV.

    Weeks 3–5

  4. Select

    Typically 3–5 candidates and a two-stage interview, with structured feedback and honest advice throughout.

    Weeks 5–8

  5. Deliver

    We manage offers, counter-offers and notice periods, then check in for six months after the start.

    Week 8 onwards
The deliverable

Every candidate, the full picture.

Shortlists arrive in HireInsight, a secure platform your hiring team can use 24/7.

Each profile includes an executive summary, video introduction, DISC and behavioural profile, competency results, pay expectations, references and CV. Most firms only do this for board roles. We do it as standard.

Fee structure

Three stages. Skin in the game on both sides.

Our fee is a percentage of the candidate’s total annual remuneration (TAR), paid in three stages in line with industry standard.

⅓

On engagement

When you instruct us. Starts the search and our exclusivity.

⅓

On shortlist

When we deliver your assessed shortlist in HireInsight.

⅓

On placement

When the candidate accepts your offer and starts.

28% of TAR up to £75,000, 30% up to £150,000 and 35% above. If a placement leaves within 8 weeks for a reason within our control, we’ll find a replacement to the value of the fee. Full terms on request.
