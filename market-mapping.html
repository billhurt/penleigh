Home / Services / Market Mapping

Talent intelligence, before you need it.

A confidential study of the people, moves and pay shaping your market, commissioned before a hire, restructure or transaction.

What it is

Who’s where, doing what, for how much.

A market map is a structured, anonymised view of the senior talent pool in your segment, region or function: who’s mobile, who’s settled, what they’re paid and where the next moves will come from.

It’s commissioned by founders, boards, PE sponsors and M&A teams. The work is always confidential, bespoke and senior-led.

When to use it

Four scenarios where mapping pays for itself.

Pre-hire

Before you commit to a search

Find out who exists, what they cost and whether your target profile is realistic before you write the brief.

PE / M&A

Due diligence on leadership

Assess a target’s senior bench: single points of failure, retention risk and who could step in after the deal.

Strategy

Build-out & expansion

Entering a new region or segment? Know the talent, pay and realistic hiring timeline before committing capital.

Succession

Quiet succession planning

Identify successors for board or C-suite roles well before any announcement, with no approaches unless you decide.

What you receive

A structured intelligence report. Yours to keep.

A confidential PDF report and candidate dataset, presented in a working session with you and your stakeholders.

Market overview

Operators, service companies and vendors, their scale, recent leadership moves and new entrants.

Senior talent profiles

Anonymised profiles covering role, tenure, background, mobility signals and pay benchmark.

Pay benchmarks

Salary, bonus, LTIP and total package ranges, from live market intelligence rather than old surveys.

Strategic observations

Where the market’s heading, who’ll be available in 6–12 months, and what it means for your next move.

How it works

From scoping call to delivered intelligence in four to six weeks.

  1. Scoping

    We agree the segment, geography, seniority and the question you need answered. Deliverable and fee are agreed in writing first.

    Week 1

  2. Research

    Desk research, then discreet conversations with our network and pay validation against live offers.

    Weeks 1–4

  3. Synthesis

    Findings consolidated into the report and profiles, drawn from the full body of evidence.

    Week 5

  4. Delivery

    A 60–90 minute working session, with the report and dataset delivered confidentially.

    Week 6

Confidential by default.

No approach identifies you as the client unless agreed in advance. Reports go to named stakeholders only, NDAs are standard, and we’re comfortable working under client-side legal frameworks.
