Before you write

Common questions.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within a working day, or sooner if it’s time-sensitive.

Do I need to commit to anything?

No. Initial scoping calls are confidential and free. We only formalise an engagement once we’ve both agreed it’s the right fit.

What if I’m not sure I need a search yet?

That’s often where the best conversations start. We’ll talk through whether a search, a market map or a few introductions would serve you best.

What if my role isn’t in midstream or subsea?

We’ll tell you directly and, where we can, point you to a specialist who’ll serve you better.

Is everything we discuss confidential?

Always, including enquiries that don’t become engagements. NDAs are standard for mapping work and available for searches.
